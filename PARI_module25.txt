
Module M25: Vendor Invoice Allocation (VIA) & Reconciliation Engine

Summary
The Vendor Invoice Allocation (VIA) system is a specialized, privacy-preserving sub-ledger and reconciliation engine within the PARI architecture. While PARI handles the anonymous transfer of funds, VIA bridges the gap between these cryptographic payments and the rigorous demands of B2B accounting, procurement, and tax compliance. It automates the 3-way matching of Purchase Orders (POs), Vendor Invoices, and PARI Payments, utilizing Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKPs) to verify that a payment obligation has been settled without exposing the underlying payer’s identity to unauthorized parties. VIA manages platform entitlements (e.g., Bloomberg, LSEG), stores critical contract metadata, and provides a direct integration layer for Accounts Payable (AP) systems like Concur. Engineered under CMMI Level 5 standards, VIA ensures data integrity, auditability, and fiscal traceability while respecting the core PARI tenet of payer anonymity.

Scope
The scope of Module M25 includes:

     Ingestion: Automated ingestion of ISO 20022 (pacs.008/009), UBL 2.1, and PDF invoices via API/SFTP.
     Parsing & Extraction: AI-driven extraction of line-item details, tax codes, and bank details.
     Cryptographic Reconciliation: Linking blinded PARI transaction hashes to invoice hashes using ZK-Proofs of Payment.
     3-Way Matching: Automated logic to match PO ↔
     Invoice
    ↔
     Payment/Receipt with configurable tolerance rules.
     Entitlement Management: Tracking software/data subscriptions (Bloomberg, Refinitiv) linked to vendor contracts.
     Tax Compliance: Input VAT validation, Intrastat reporting, and cross-border tax rule application.
     ERP Integration: Bi-directional sync with SAP, Oracle, and Concur for master data and journal entries.
     Reporting: Executive dashboards for working capital, vendor performance, and accruals.


Business Case

Modern enterprises suffer from a "Procurement-Payment Disconnect." Traditional payment rails (SWIFT/SEPA) carry identifying data, creating privacy risks and security liabilities, while privacy-preserving cash or crypto lacks the structured metadata required for B2B accounting (tax deduction, reconciliation, audit). Manual reconciliation is slow (2-3 weeks), error-prone (up to 15% discrepancy rates), and expensive.

VIA solves this by acting as a cryptographic translator. It allows a company to pay a vendor anonymously via PARI (protecting supply chain secrecy) while simultaneously generating a verifiable, audit-proof proof that settles the specific invoice in the ERP. This eliminates the privacy-tax trade-off. Furthermore, by automating the ingestion and 3-way matching, VIA reduces Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and manual processing costs by over 70%. It ensures that VAT recoverability is never jeopardized by payment anonymity, a critical requirement for EU B2B commerce.
